# Prompt do cabeçalho

Ferramenta: imagegen integrada, geração nova. Arquivo final: assets/cyberpunk/hero.png.

Create a finished premium ultra-wide GitHub profile banner, 3:1 landscape aspect ratio, inspired by the graphic design of Cyberpunk 2077. This is a personal developer identity for Pedro Zampronha, not official game branding. Must feel unmistakably like the game: vivid almost fluorescent flat industrial yellow #fcee0a, dense black, electric cyan #00e5ff, sparse scarlet glitch cuts.
Composition: left 60% a boldly composed flat yellow field with a few purposeful sharp cutout corners, tiny technical linework, subtle battered print texture and three very short displaced glitch slices. Left has huge custom angular italic condensed black lettering, aggressive tech wordmark typography, exactly two lines 'PEDRO' and 'ZAMPRONHA'. It must be spectacular, legible and fit with generous margins, not a generic sans-serif word. Small monospaced black text above 'PHBR63 // DEV PROFILE'. Compact exact subtitle below 'SOFTWARE · INTEGRAÇÕES · DADOS'.
Right 40%: richly detailed cinematic Night City inspired urban skyline at rainy blue hour, enormous megabuildings, elevated streets, dense cables and luminous cyan/magenta neon reflected on wet surfaces. No people, no vehicles as focal subjects, no unrelated fantasy, no castles. Use a severe diagonal division with a black/cyan stepped frame between the two halves, and let some skyline silhouette overlap its edge. The palette balances big flat yellow graphic areas with richly rendered midnight city.
No extra text, no official Cyberpunk logo, no invented experience numbers, progress bars, fake metrics, watermarks, browser/device mockup, rounded cards or translucent glass panels. Render the actual production banner only. Save the result locally and report its absolute file path for repository integration.
